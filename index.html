About

I recently graduated from the University of Oxford with an MEng in Engineering Science (2:1). My projects there ranged from understanding scenes through AR glasses to separating music into its component sources using transformers.

Alongside my studies I've done research in neural rendering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, worked on SLAM tooling at the Oxford Robotics Institute, and developed machine vision techniques for anti-air systems at Quell. I'm now co-founding Autotone, which uses machine learning to estimate guitar effect settings from audio.

Outside of engineering, I sing and play guitar in a local band, Head Height, and was president of the St Anne's College football club.

Dual British and Irish national. DBS Level 2 security clearance.
