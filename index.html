Projects

Autotone

Autotone is a start-up using machine learning to estimate guitar effect parameters directly from audio, so that a player can recreate a tone they hear without tweaking settings by hand. It grew out of my third-year project on music source separation, and we are developing hybrid waveform-spectrogram transformer models to embed guitar tone.

Master's thesis

My master's thesis explored egocentric semantic scene understanding using Meta's Project Aria AR glasses. I trained an LSTM to detect hand-object interactions from hand- and eye-tracking data, working out what a wearer is doing from where they look and how they move their hands. The thesis was awarded 78%.

Synthetic data generation

During an internship at a defence technology start-up, I worked on machine vision and investigated whether synthetic data from a simulator could improve model performance.
